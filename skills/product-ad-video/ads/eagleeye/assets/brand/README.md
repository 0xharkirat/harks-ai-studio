# EagleEye brand files

All files are official SSW EagleEye art. Nobody redrew them.

- `logo.svg`, `logo-light.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `icon.png`: from the portal repo, `SSW.EagleEye/src/SSW.EagleEye.Portal/public/`.
- `site-logo.svg`: from ssweagleeye.com (`assets.tina.io/.../EagleEye/Logo.svg`), the same art as `logo-dark.svg`.
- `mark-light.svg`, `mark-dark.svg`: the `< eye >` mark. They are the first 4 paths of `logo.svg` / `logo-dark.svg` with a cropped viewBox, unchanged.
- `eye-light.svg`, `eye-dark.svg`: the eye only (the first 2 paths), with a cropped viewBox, unchanged.
