# EagleEye: treatment

## The idea

Your AI sees 1 repo, but EagleEye sees the whole org: every mailbox and every repo, inside your own Azure.

## Campaign line

"See your whole org. *Clearly.*"

## Call to action

"Try EagleEye today." with the red "Try the live demo" button (demo.ssweagleeye.com), "or", "Ask your favourite AI agent 🤖", and ssweagleeye.com.
The QR code opens the demo with `utm_source=video&utm_medium=qr&utm_campaign=anthem`.

## Style bible

- Palette: SSW red `#CC4141` as the only accent. The dark world is the site's `#1F1F1F`. The light world is zinc white.
- Type: Inter, as ssweagleeye.com serves it. Code in IBM Plex Mono.
- The 2 worlds: dark for the Developer (the AI prompt, the agent), light for the Team Lead (the portal, the reports).
- Motif: the eye mark. It answers the problem and sits on the end card.
- Real UI: every feature claim shows the real demo portal, with Northwind Traders data.
- Music: code-made, calm, 90 BPM, with the EagleEye sonic logo on the title and on the end card.
- Sound: SSW TV pops, clicks and typing.
