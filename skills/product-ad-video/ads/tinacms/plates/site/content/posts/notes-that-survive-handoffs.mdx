When a project changes hands, the note is all that is left of the context. Most handoff notes fail because they describe what was done, not what is next.

## The three questions

1. **Where are we?** One short paragraph, no history.
2. **What is blocked?** Name the person or the decision that blocks it.
3. **What happens next?** The very next action, with an owner.

## A tiny template

Start every handoff with the same three headings. Fill them in under five minutes, then ask a teammate to read it cold. If they have a question, the note is not done.
