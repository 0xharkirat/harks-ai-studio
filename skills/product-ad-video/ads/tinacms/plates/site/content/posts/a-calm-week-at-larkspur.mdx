Larkspur Studio is a ten person team that ships every Thursday. We cut our meetings by half last year and shipped more, not less.

## Monday to Friday

On Monday, everyone reads the plan note. On Wednesday, we update it. On Thursday, we ship. On Friday, we write what we learned and go home on time.
