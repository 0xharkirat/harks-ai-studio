Keyword search is fast and wrong about half the time. For Fieldnote 3.0 we added a second pass that reads the question and the notes together.

## How ranking works

- **Meaning** matters most. A note about "pricing page ownership" matches "who owns pricing".
- **Recency** breaks ties, because last week's decision beats last year's.
- **Authors** count a little, so notes from the space owner rank first.

The result is a list you can trust without scrolling.
