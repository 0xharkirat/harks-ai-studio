# YakShaver anthem: treatment

A 76 s music-driven ad for YakShaver, with no voice, built in code on the TinaCMS anthem engine.
The facts and their sources are in `docs/marketing-brief.md`.
The scene script is `docs/cuts/anthem.md`.

## The idea

**You talk through the bug, and YakShaver writes the PBI that the developer wants.**

- Campaign line: **"The perfect PBI. In seconds."** (the site hero, cut to 2 lines).
- Brand line: **"See it. Shave it. Solve it."** (the site's 3 steps).
- Hook: **"Bug reports are yak shaving."** It says what the name means and names the pain in 5 words.
- Call to action: the "Try it now" button and **yakshaver.ai**, with "or Shave it in Microsoft Teams" under the button.

## The 2 worlds

- The developer's world is dark: the site greys `#222222` and `#131313`, code, the GitHub issue.
- Everyone else's world is light: off-white with soft blobs of the YakShaver gradient.
  It holds northwind365.com, SSW's public demo site, where the bug lives, and the YakShaver recorder.
- The film switches between them to show whose world each scene is.

## The cast

- **The developer**: 3D cartoon, Jack's style. Strip `dev`: typing, then a thumbs-up.
- **The reporter** (everyone else): 3D cartoon, Jack's style, a woman (the CC0 scream is a woman's voice). Strip `editor-stressed`: a frown at the questions, then hands on the head. Strip `editor-happy`: a smile, then a fist pump.
- **Jack the Yak**: the mascot, in the llama's role. He answers the pain at 2x size, and he relaxes on the end card. New poses come from the official character sheet with the brand guide's own prompt.

## Ad rules

1. Hook in the first 2 s. Show the logo on the title cut at about 0:05.
   Keep the corner logo top left at 80% until the end card.
2. Sound off first: the words and pictures tell the story.
3. 2 to 5 words per line, sentence case, a full stop. 1 gradient word per headline.
4. Show the real YakShaver UI wherever the film claims a feature.
   The captures come from a real shave on a demo repo: YakShaver Anywhere and the GitHub issue that it writes.
5. 1 call to action.
6. Claims from the brief only. No time numbers, no "open source", no third-party logos (platform names in type).

## Style bible

- Type: Inter only. Headlines 700 with tight tracking; UI 400 to 600.
- Accent: YakShaver red `#CC4141`. The gradient word uses the site hero gradient (red > lilac > pink).
- The YakShaver gradient stays soft and below 1/3 of the screen.
- Motifs:
  1. **The transcript.** Spoken words appear as text, word by word. It shows what YakShaver does: it listens.
  2. **The stage list.** The real workflow stages tick to done, 1 per beat.
  3. **The scissors.** The app's own scissors confetti bursts when the issue lands.
  4. **Jack.** Official reaction heads and new poses from the sheet only.
- Motion and pace: the engine's rules and the TinaCMS review lessons (90 BPM, 1 request every 2 s, no empty holds).
